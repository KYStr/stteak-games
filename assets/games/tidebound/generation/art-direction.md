# Tidebound original art direction

Created 2026-10-25 by the Tidebound world-art Maker. No image generation, photographs, reference models, commercial characters, or third-party art assets were used.

A sunlit miniature archipelago in deep turquoise, warm linen, sea-glass green, and coral enamel. Angular terraced islands contrast with legible timber boats. The same sunlight comes from the northwest throughout. The vessel is an original small lateen-inspired exploration craft with three visibly different rigs. The seven islands use hand-authored positions from world.js, and independent generated geometry from models.js.

All geometry is editable JavaScript, with glTF 2.0 binary interchange, separate collider files, primitive UV0, standard PBR material factors, and a seven-bone skinned voyager with DockIdle, Swim, DockWave clips. No sprite substitutes are used for core content.
