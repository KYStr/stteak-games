# Hollow Crown original procedural asset record

Created 2026-10-25 by the Hollow Crown scene-assets Maker assistant. Tools: editable JavaScript, Three.js 0.180.0, bundled GLTFExporter. No image generation, downloaded models, sampled textures, or third-party character references were used. The materials and silhouettes follow the project's original brief: coal black, ivory enamel, restrained ember red, ruined stone court, solitary spear guardian.

The editable source is `sources/kit.mjs`; `recipes/export-assets.mjs` copies it into the game's fingerprinted runtime and exports GLB models with the same geometry, named joints and clips. Run with the locked Three.js dependency installed in `games/hollow-crown/`. The exporter records real SHA-256 hashes and byte counts in `manifest.json`.

Characters use rigid mesh pieces parented to actual Three.js Bone joints, not a stretched whole-character mesh. GLB exports preserve the node hierarchy and quaternion animation channels. Root motion is disabled: simulation moves the actor root, animations move joints. Every character shares `root/hips/spine`, arms and legs; `weapon-mount` is under the right hand. The beetle adds six separately animated limb joints. Idle, run, light, charge, block, dodge, hurt, death, telegraph, attack, recover are the stable clip names. Light/attack impact marker is 0.16/0.18 seconds; gameplay owns authoritative attack windows.

Units are metres, +Y up and +Z forward, pivot is on the floor below the pelvis. Colliders are stored separately in `collision-proxies.json`. The collision specification is a reuse contract; the current game rules remain authoritative. Materials use UV-equipped Three.js primitive geometries, explicit roughness/metalness and optional emissive factors. No material contains baked lighting or text. Stone uses three original deterministic 256×256 base-color, OpenGL normal and roughness PNG maps from `recipes/stone-surfaces.mjs`, generated using periodic multiscale noise, fine grain and mineral fissures. They are loaded locally at runtime and embedded in the corresponding GLB materials; uncompressed RGBA plus mipmaps totals approximately 1 MiB. Stone blocks have real bevel geometry, columns have stacked stone joints, walls have separate courses, and rubble is reusable geometry.

The guardian has an ivory variant and a soot variant through the `skin` parameter. Five enemy silhouettes are a sword infantry, spear lancer, crossbow carrier, six-limbed ember beetle, and rectangular heavy shield bearer. Two bosses add enlarged armour, a seven-point crown and, respectively, a long spear or a burning censer staff. A modular ruin kit contains floor tile, wall, two pillar LODs, candelabrum, torn banner and altar. Current gameplay IDs map soldier→infantry, spear→lancer, crossbow→arbalest, beetle→cinderbug, shield→shieldguard, regent→crown, arbiter→bellwarden.

Scene rendering batches repeat floor stones, masonry, pillars, candle components and ember particles using InstancedMesh; distant pillars use an actual low geometry LOD. Animated rigid Bone mesh world matrices are batched by shared geometry/material each frame, preserving actual skeletal motion while reducing draw calls. The detailed eight-character Maker sample measured 129 draw calls and 26,236 visible triangles; this is an asset budget observation, not a hardware performance pass. Low quality disables the dynamic shadow map, caps DPR at 1 and reduces decorative embers. High quality caps DPR at 1.6 and uses one 1024² directional shadow map. Foreground obstacle materials fade when their projected bounds cover the player. Attack warnings are separate depth-independent ground geometry so foreground masonry cannot erase the warning. Player attack clip timing scales to the authoritative activeStart; enemy attack clips enter at their impact pose when the active damage state begins. Held charge and released charge are distinguished by the read-only player.attack field.

Room themes share the same kit with explicit composition and palette changes: ivory has warm pale stone, ash adds broken fragments, ember adds four perimeter braziers and restrained glowing floor fissures, blue has cool lighting and shallow reflective water patches, and crown adds a ceremonial banner line with paired braziers. Decorations stay outside the principal combat lanes; active warnings remain distinct geometry above the floor. The desktop camp camera closes in on the guardian and places it to the right of the menu. Gameplay camera framing remains unchanged. The unlocked `cinder` skin uses the same dark armour variant as `ember`.
