# Aetherwake original asset kit

Created 2026-10-25 for stteak by the Maker asset assistant. No image generator,
stock model, image search, external audio recording, or commercial character was
used. The authored geometry and music are deterministic JavaScript recipes.

Art direction: layered teal basalt islands above a pale cloud sea; moss terraces,
ochre guide markings, warm brass astronomical instruments, cream sail cloth and
a small navy/cream aviator. The major silhouettes remain three dimensional under
camera rotation. Solid PBR materials expose generated UVs for later texture work;
there are no baked shadows or painted substitutes for world geometry.

Six island variants share a circular top collision contract and use a reusable
terrace/cliff construction. Their strata, cliff columns, broken arches, inlays,
and vegetation parameters vary by stable index. Plant geometry is instanced.
Each has two genuine geometry LOD levels with the switch at 145 metres. Both
retain their unique peripheral landmark: sail shelter, reed gate, broken bridge,
lightning frame, library colonnade, or dawn dome. The play disk stays unobstructed;
uprights sit on outlying rock buttresses beyond 1.04 times the island radius.
Near LODs add mineral ground patches, inset slabs, larger windgrass and seed heads.

The pilot uses an eight-bone rigid weighted skeleton with named clips idle, run,
jump, glide, land. Root motion is disabled; gameplay owns position. The frame,
cloth, suspension lines and pilot are geometry. The glider appears for glide.

Three observatories use distinct silhouettes: weather vane, solar disc and
armillary sphere. Twelve fragments vary in core polyhedron, core proportions,
ring orientation, tick count and inner ring. Matching original SVG UI glyphs are
auxiliary interface art and never replace the models.

The audio score is an original sparse D pentatonic phrase, 75 BPM, with sine and
triangle oscillators. UI and movement cues use defined frequency sequences and
short attack/exponential release envelopes. Wind uses deterministic filtered
noise. Loop rendering wraps note tails across the boundary to avoid a hard cut.
